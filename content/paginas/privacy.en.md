- **Who we are:** Trader Binário (traderbinario.com.br) is run by XMARKETING DIGITAL LTDA (CNPJ 29.184.535/0001-41), São Paulo, Brazil. Contact and data protection officer: Cristiano Pereira, contato@cristianopereira.com.br.
- **What we collect on the English pages:** no forms, no e-mail, no chat. We only collect technical access data (IP address, browser, date and time) for security, and — **only if you click "Accept" on the cookie notice** — Google Analytics cookies to count visits and clicks in aggregate, never with your name or e-mail. Without consent, Google receives only basic cookieless signals (Google Consent Mode).
- **Who we share with:** Vercel (hosting, United States) and Google (Analytics and Tag Manager, United States, only with your consent).
- **Retention:** access logs for 6 months; Analytics data for 14 months.
- **Your rights (Brazilian LGPD and GDPR principles):** access, correction, deletion, withdrawing consent at any time via "Cookie preferences" in the footer, and complaining to your data protection authority. Write to the e-mail above; we reply within 15 days.
- **Links:** Telegram, Deriv and other external links have their own policies. Some are affiliate links.
