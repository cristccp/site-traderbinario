**The Ideal Trade terminal now has two charts: Light, which was already there, and Advanced, with [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) charts.** You pick one in the chart selector, and your choice is saved to your account.

A lot of people think they need a chart packed with features to trade well. You don't. What protects your balance is money management. But if you already have a method and like to study price calmly, you just got one more tool.

In this article I show you what changes between the two charts, when to use each one and how to turn on Advanced.

## What the *Advanced* chart is

Advanced uses TradingView's **Advanced Charts** library, the same family of charts many traders already know. Inside Ideal Trade, it gets the live price of the same asset you are trading.

What you get:

- **100+ built-in indicators**, such as moving averages, RSI, MACD and Bollinger Bands.
- **Drawing tools:** trend lines, support and resistance, Fibonacci, rectangles and notes.
- **More timeframes:** besides 1 tick, 1, 5 and 15 minutes and 1 hour, also 2, 3, 10 and 30 minutes, 2, 4 and 8 hours and 1 day.
- **Your trade marks:** entry, entry price and result show on the chart, for manual and robot trades.

The drawings and indicators you set up are saved in your browser. Come back tomorrow and everything is still there.

## Light or Advanced: *which one*

Both show the same price. The difference is what each one does well.

| Situation | Chart to use |
|---|---|
| You are starting out and want clarity | Light |
| The robot is running | Light (or Room mode) |
| Simpler computer or phone | Light |
| You want to analyze with indicators and drawings | Advanced |
| You want bigger timeframes, in hours or days | Advanced |

**Light is the default.** It loads fast and is light on your browser. That is why it is the one to use when the robot is on: less running on the screen, less chance of getting in the way.

**Advanced is for manual analysis.** If you already know what you are looking for on the chart, it gives you the tools. If you don't, more indicators just mean more noise.

## More indicators is not *more accuracy*

Here is the trap. With 100 indicators at hand, the temptation is to fill the screen and wait for all of them to agree.

That doesn't happen. And when it seems to, the entry is already gone.

- **Pick a few indicators** and understand what each one measures.
- **Draw only what changes your decision:** support, resistance and the trend. The rest is decoration.
- **The chart doesn't decide your trade size.** Your money management does. See [money management for binary options](/en/blog/binary-options-money-management).

A beautiful chart with bad money management still blows accounts.

## How to turn on *Advanced*

:::na-pratica
1. **Create your free account** on [Ideal Trade](https://cristianopereira.com.br/plataforma) or log in to the one you have. The interface is in Portuguese for now.
2. **Connect your broker account** and start on the **demo** account.
3. **Open the trading terminal.**
4. **At the top of the chart, choose Advanced** ("Avançado"). The first time, it takes a few seconds to load.
5. **Set up your chart:** a few indicators, the drawings that matter and the timeframe of your method.
6. **Turning on the robot?** Switch back to Light ("Leve") or use Room mode.
:::
